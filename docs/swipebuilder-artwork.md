# SwipeBuilder creator artwork

Three original monochrome portraits were generated with the built-in image generation tool for this portfolio. They depict fictional adults sharing food and travel experiences, not real customer campaigns. The page presents them as vertical creator video references in the animated notebook; there is no banner. Captions and layout are authored in React. Full heads remain visible with top-aligned framing.

## food creator

Asset: `dist/assets/swipebuilder-food-creator.png`

### Generation prompt

Create one original photorealistic monochrome portrait image for a short-form creator video card in a portfolio. Vertical 9:16 framing, candid TikTok-style talking-to-camera still, rich grayscale, natural soft light, realistic skin and fabric texture, subtle photographic grain matching a charcoal-on-paper website. A fictional adult creator, engaged mid-sentence, warmly expressive, direct eye contact with the camera, natural conversational hand gesture. Frame from waist or mid-torso upward, keep the entire head and hair fully visible with generous headroom and side margins. Face in upper third, uncluttered lower third for separately added captions. No embedded text, logos, watermarks, app interface, advertising, shopping bags, packaged products, or product placement. This is a creator sharing food or travel experiences, not selling merchandise. Subject: a woman in her late twenties with dark wavy shoulder-length hair and warm medium skin, sitting at a small neighborhood noodle restaurant, excitedly telling the viewer about this hidden food spot. A modest bowl of noodles rests on the table at the lower edge, she gestures toward the kitchen rather than holding an item to sell. Cozy window-side table, blurred restaurant activity behind her, a lived-in local atmosphere.
