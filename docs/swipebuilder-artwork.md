## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a new high-quality wide landscape graphite pencil illustration for an existing handwritten portfolio website, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. It illustrates SwipeBuilder, a tool for collecting ad inspiration, organizing creative boards and writing new advertising scripts. An elegant tactile creative research desk seen at a shallow isometric angle: loose paper ad cards and miniature browser windows on the left featuring delicately drawn everyday objects (a ceramic skincare jar, headphones, a travel mug); in the center these become a beautifully organized physical pinboard with 6 paper clippings connected by thin hand-drawn pencil lines; at the right an open notebook with loosely sketched lines of script and a long graphite pencil resting diagonally beside it. Fine precise pencil crosshatching, realistic paper edges, charming restrained editorial illustration, detailed textures and soft graphite shadows. Monochrome only, shades of charcoal and warm gray. Continuous light warm gray paper background (#eeedeb) fading at all outer edges, generous breathing room. Art fills a wide horizontal band, all main objects fully visible. No people, no mascots, no logos, no readable text, no colored accents, no hard frame or white rectangular background. Match the feeling of an intricate pencil sketch of browser windows on a quiet paper portfolio.
