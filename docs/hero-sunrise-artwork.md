# Sunrise fishing illustration

Current asset: `dist/assets/seymur-fishing-natural.png`.

A single cohesive graphite illustration based on the user’s supplied photographs. It retains the full-body seated fishing pose, rod, rock, natural athletic proportions, and upward three-quarter gaze. The face is drawn in the same graphite medium as the body, with relaxed slightly open eyes. The rejected photographic face overlay, its masks, and source JPEG were removed from the current site. Earlier assets remain available as historical variants.

The approximately 274-pixel-tall figure sits within a 540 × 375 SVG scene with rising blue and sage-green mountains and a warm golden sun. The characters, fishing equipment, and TV-static effect retain their monochrome palette. The hero reserves the opening viewport for the name and scene, with Weaszel beginning below the fold. TV snow, scan lines and a tracking band bridge a roughly four-second transformation from Astro. Equally spaced SVG contour points interpolate from Astro’s outline to the seated figure, while a displacement filter gently warps both figures and the illustration gradually resolves. The rod stretches into place along with the silhouette. All distortion and reveal clipping are removed for the clear illustration. On return, static and displacement reappear as the same contour contracts down into Astro over about three seconds. The manual return control also runs this reverse transformation once the portrait has resolved; early cancellation and reduced motion remain immediate. The SVG fishing line joins the rod to a gently bobbing float. The 18-second scene retains collected facts, pauses when offscreen, and offers a still manual scene with reduced motion. The first automatic scene starts after roughly 11 visible seconds; subsequent scenes wait 35–55 seconds. Desktop and mobile framing preserve the full figure and rod tip.

The character image uses a silhouette clip and normal compositing to stay opaque over the field. The separate environment uses multiply blending and soft edge masks to meet the paper background. The face is part of the same bitmap as the body; there is no separate photo layer.
